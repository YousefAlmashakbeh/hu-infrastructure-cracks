About the Dataset

Built for the global research community

A systematic, high-quality collection of concrete crack images captured at The Hashemite University campus using a professional protocol.

10–15 images per crack

Far, medium, close-up from 3 angles, scale reference, 3 lighting conditions.

Standardized field data

Type, severity, dimensions, surface conditions and full environment metadata per crack.

Open-source forever

CC BY 4.0 — free to use for research, AI training, or commercial applications with credit.

Data Collection Protocol

Standardized for scientific accuracy

Every crack follows a strict protocol. Engineer: Razan Al-Majali. Camera: Canon EOS R2 DSLR.

01 / Field Data

Crack Characteristics

Full physical measurement before photography.

  • Type: transverse, longitudinal, diagonal
  • Severity: moderate or severe
  • Width and length in mm
  • Surface: dry, wet, or dusty
02 / Photography

Canon EOS R2 DSLR

Professional camera settings logged per crack.

  • Resolution: 4000×3000 px
  • Zoom: 1x standard
  • Focus: Auto mode
  • Stability: handheld
03 / Photo Types

10–15 Images Per Crack

Multi-view acquisition for AI training robustness.

  • Far view: 2–5 m context
  • Close-up: 0°, +30°, −30°
  • Scale reference with ruler
  • 3 lighting conditions

What you CANNOT do
