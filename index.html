About the Dataset

Built for the global research community

A systematic, high-quality collection of concrete crack images captured at The Hashemite University campus using a professional protocol designed for AI model training.

10–15 images per crack

Far, medium, close-up from 3 angles, scale reference, 3 lighting conditions, and negative sample.

Standardized field data

Every crack includes type, severity, width, length, surface conditions, and environment metadata.

Open-source forever

Data Collection Protocol

Standardized for scientific accuracy

Every crack follows a strict photo acquisition protocol ensuring consistency and usability for AI training.

01 / Field Data

Crack Characteristics

Each crack is documented with full physical measurements before photography.

  • Type: longitudinal, transverse, diagonal, hairline
  • Severity: minor, moderate, severe
  • Width and length in mm
  • Surface: dry, wet, or dusty
  • Corrosion and water presence noted
02 / Photography

Camera Settings Logged

All camera parameters recorded for full reproducibility.

  • Camera: DSLR high-resolution
  • Resolution: 4000×3000 px minimum
  • Zoom: 1x / 2x / 5x logged
  • Focus: auto and manual
  • Flash and stability recorded
03 / Photo Types

10–15 Images Per Crack

Systematic multi-view acquisition for robust AI training data.

  • Far view: 2–5 m context shot
  • Medium view: 1 m front
  • Close-up: 0°, +30°, −30°
  • Scale reference with ruler
  • 3 lighting: sun, shadow, flash
  • Negative sample included
File Naming Convention

Consistent naming for every image

[CrackID]_[Type]_[Distance]_[Angle]_[Lighting]_[Number].jpg
Example: CR01_close_30cm_0deg_sun_01.jpg

Community Contribution

Help grow the global dataset

Submit your own crack records from any infrastructure project worldwide. All contributions are reviewed and credited.

Submit a Crack Record

Drop crack photos here or click to browse

JPG, PNG — min 4000×3000 px recommended

Contribution guidelines

Photos must follow the protocol: far, medium, close-up (0°, ±30°), scale, lighting, negative. Minimum 10 images per crack. All images must be original work.

Review process

All submissions are reviewed by the research team at The Hashemite University within 3–5 business days. Accepted contributions are credited in the dataset.

Credit & attribution

Contributors are listed in the dataset README and on this website. Your institution will be credited in every crack record you submit.
