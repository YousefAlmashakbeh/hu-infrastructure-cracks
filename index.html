Data Collection Protocol

Standardized for scientific accuracy

Every crack follows a strict protocol. Engineer: Razan Al-Majali. Camera: Canon EOS R2 DSLR.

01 / Field Data

Crack Characteristics

Full physical measurement before photography.

  • Type: transverse, longitudinal, diagonal
  • Severity: moderate or severe
  • Width and length in mm
  • Surface condition: dry / wet / dusty
  • Corrosion and water presence noted
02 / Camera

Canon EOS R2 DSLR

Professional camera settings logged per crack.

  • Resolution: 4000×3000 px minimum
  • Zoom: 1x (standard)
  • Focus: Auto mode
  • Stability: handheld
  • Orientation: landscape / portrait
  • Distance: estimated
03 / Photo Types

10–15 Images Per Crack

Multi-view acquisition for AI training robustness.

  • Far view: 2–5 m context
  • Medium view: 1 m front
  • Close-up: 0°, +30°, −30°
  • Scale reference with ruler
  • Lighting: sunny / shadow / flash
  • Negative sample included
